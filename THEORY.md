# Minimal Theory Statement

Relational Substrate Mechanics begins from relations and transformations rather than treating named objects, states, instants, or boundaries as self-sufficient primitives.

The empirical hypothesis examined here is that coordinate duration can be recovered from intrinsic state-reconfiguration arc length through a conversion fixed once:

```text
dt = kappa * d_tau
```

This identity is a hypothesis until its operational definitions uniquely determine `d_tau`, its calibration fixes `kappa` without leakage, and its predictions survive unseen regimes without additional fitted quantities.

Conceptual completeness, numerical agreement, reproducibility, and empirical validity are separate claims. None substitutes for another.
