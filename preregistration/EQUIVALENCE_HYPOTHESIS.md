# Global Equivalence Hypothesis

`H_EQ`: For every operationally defined domain tested, M is observationally equivalent to the declared Newtonian/classical theory within frozen numerical and experimental resolution.

This is a legitimate, publishable outcome.

If `H_EQ` survives all tests, the permitted conclusion is:

- M may be a reformulation, representation, diagnostic language, or computational organization of known mechanics;
- no new physical predictive power has been demonstrated;
- conceptual or computational value must be evaluated separately from claims of new physics.

`H_EQ` is not rejected merely because M uses different symbols or interpretations. Rejection requires a preregistered, resolvable prediction difference followed by data satisfying the decision matrix.
